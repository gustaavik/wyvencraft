# Wyvencraft trademark policy

The source in this repository is open — see [LICENSE-MIT](LICENSE-MIT) and
[LICENSE-APACHE](LICENSE-APACHE). The *name* is not part of that grant.

Neither license conveys any right to the Wyvencraft marks. Apache-2.0 says so
outright in section 6; MIT is silent on trademarks, which is why this file
exists.

## The marks

- **Wyvencraft**, as a name for this game, this engine, or a service around them
- The Wyvencraft logo and other project artwork
- The domains **wyvencraft.com** and **auth.wyvencraft.com**

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your thing is built on.** "Built on the Wyvencraft engine", "uses
  `wyven-voxel`", "a Wyvencraft mod", "compatible with Wyvencraft" — accurate,
  descriptive references are fine and always will be.
- **Run a server.** If you host a game server, or run your own identity server
  for your own community, you may say it is a Wyvencraft server. Call it
  *unofficial* if there is any chance of confusion — and there usually is.
- **Talk about the project.** Reviews, tutorials, videos, articles, forks
  discussed by name. Nominative use needs no permission.

## What needs permission

- Naming your fork, product, service or company **Wyvencraft**, or a name close
  enough to be mistaken for it.
- Using the logo as the icon or branding of something that is not this project.
- Anything that suggests your work is official, endorsed, or affiliated —
  including a domain, a store page, or a launcher that reads that way.
- Selling accounts, access, or a product under the Wyvencraft name.

A fork is welcome. A fork *called Wyvencraft* is not, because the name is how
players tell the official network apart from everything else.

## Why the split

The licenses are permissive on purpose: take the engine, build your own game,
sell it, do not ask. The mark is reserved on purpose: a player who buys an
account should be able to tell what they are buying it from. Those two goals do
not conflict — they are separated exactly here.

Questions, or a use you are unsure about: open an issue.
